# Expected: Neap

Written before the first full render.

## The audit

- Profile: dark.
- Mean luminance: low, about 10 to 15 per cent.
- Loudness range: narrow, because it is speech-led (the narrated floor is 2.5 LU); the loudest moment is the close after the last word.
- Longest hold: the end card, about 4 seconds.

## A first-time viewer

- What they can say the film is about after one viewing: an app that tells you, for your harbour, the minute the tide stops.
- The moment they will remember: the light stopping dead at the top of the tide, and the sea going quiet with it.
