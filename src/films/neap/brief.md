# Brief: Neap

## Who it is for

People who launch, moor or cross a harbour bar in small boats: sailors, kayakers, swimmers. Neap is
a made-up app; this is the kit's narrated example.

## The one thing they must remember

Neap tells you when the water stops, to the minute, for your harbour.

## What they should feel

Calm and certain. The sea has its own rhythm, and someone has written it down for them.

## Length, shape and where it plays

About 20 seconds, 16:9 at 1080p, sound on, with burned-in captions so it also works muted. A narrated
film: the voice leads, the picture keys on its words, the score sits under it.

## Must include, must avoid

Include the time the water stops (slack water) as the hero, and the "AI-generated voice" credit.
Avoid a phone or app screen (Neap is made up, so it has no real interface to show), maps of real
places, and any claim about safety.
