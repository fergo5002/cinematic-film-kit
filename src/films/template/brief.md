# Brief: Template ("One clock")

This is the starter film. `npm run new-film -- <id>` copies this folder and gives the copy a blank
brief, treatment and expected; these three files show what filled ones look like.

## Who it is for

Anyone opening the kit for the first time, and the agent working with them.

## The one thing they must remember

Picture and sound share one clock: every moment lives in `beatmap.ts`, and both the scenes and the
score read it.

## What they should feel

Calm, then a small click of satisfaction when the hand comes home on the hit.

## Length, shape and where it plays

9 seconds, 1920 x 1080 at 30 fps, sound on. A demo inside the kit, not a public film.

## Must include, must avoid

Include a shader wash, a line that draws itself, kinetic type in the bundled faces and a score
made from the beatmap. Avoid any brand, any stock look, and any motion that is not on the beat.
