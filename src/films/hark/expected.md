# Expected answers for the cold explain-back

Never shown to the reviewer. Compare their answers with this afterwards.

- **What is sold, to whom.** A phone app for iPhone and Android (Hark) that listens to birdsong and names the birds, for
  people who hear birds in the morning and want to know which ones.
- **Claims, in order.** It names every bird that sings. It separates birds singing at the same
  time. It keeps a diary of which birds you heard on which mornings.
- **Order of events.** A card listening in a dark wood; a robin named; five more birds named and
  flying out to the trees as dawn breaks; a pause; all of them at once, pulled apart into six
  labelled rows; a month of mornings with a 19-morning streak; the robin flies up, sings, and its
  song becomes the word "hark"; "Know who's singing."
- **What a viewer should remember.** The birds flying out of the card, or the song turning into
  the word.
