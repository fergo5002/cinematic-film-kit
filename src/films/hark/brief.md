# Brief: Hark, "Who's singing"

**This is the kit's worked example.** Hark is a made-up brand: there is no such app, and every
name, place and number in the film is invented for it.

- **Product.** Hark, a phone app. Hold it up at dawn and it names every bird that is singing, then
  keeps a diary of your mornings.
- **Audience.** People who hear birds every morning and have no idea which ones they are. Not
  birders: they already know.
- **The one action.** Want to try it tomorrow morning.
- **Format.** 22 seconds, 16:9, 1920x1080, 24 fps, sound on. Made for a launch page and social
  feeds, so it must work muted too: the type carries the claim, the picture carries the proof.
- **Mode.** Free. Everything drawn, animated and scored in code.
- **Claims, in order.** It names every bird that sings. It can pick six birds apart when they all
  sing at once. It remembers your mornings.
- **Close.** "Know who's singing."
- **Assumptions made without asking.** A spring morning in a back garden in north-west Europe,
  so six common garden birds; dawn at about 05:42; the app has a dark interface because people
  use it at five in the morning.
