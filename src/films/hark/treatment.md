# Treatment

120 BPM at 24 fps: a beat is 12 frames (0.5 s), a bar 48 frames (2 s). 22 s, 11 bars. Every time
below comes from `beatmap.ts`.

| Time | Picture | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 2 s | Close on the Hark card in a pre-dawn wood. "Hark names every bird that sings." rises on the left. A robin's song arrives at the card's right edge as faint dots; at 1.5 s Hark names it: the song is traced in robin orange and a "Robin" tag lands. | Air in the leaves, a low open chord. The robin's phrase. A glass note on the name. |
| 2 to 7 s | The camera eases back to the whole wood as the robin flies out of the card to its branch. | 7.5 to 12 s | The camera pushes in on the card. All six sing at once: a dense knot of colour. From 8.5 s the knot pulls apart into six labelled lanes, one bird a beat, each lane its own song's shape. | A deep low bloom; all six birds at once; each starts a new phrase as its lane separates. |
| 12 to 16 s | The lanes fold into one column of six dots: today. A month of May mornings fills in backwards from today. "19 mornings running" marks the wren's streak. | The chord settles lower. Birds further off. A patter of tiny ticks as the mornings fill. |
| 16 to 19.5 s | The robin leaves its branch and the camera follows it up into the morning sky. A low bloom, two glass notes and a wide warm chord as the word forms. |
| 19.5 to 22 s | "Know who's singing." | The chord rings out; silence for the last half second. |
